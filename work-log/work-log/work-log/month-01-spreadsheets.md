# Month 01 — Data Analysis with Spreadsheets

## Summary

After completing the Data & AI Literacy Foundation, I moved into **Data Analysis with Spreadsheets**.

This stage introduced me to the practical process of transforming raw data into reliable information and useful business insights. I learned how spreadsheets can be used not only for storing data, but also for cleaning, analyzing, visualizing, and interpreting information.

## Skills Developed

During this stage, I developed skills in:

* Transforming raw data into a more useful and structured format.
* Cleaning and preparing data for analysis.
* Performing descriptive statistics.
* Applying statistical analysis techniques.
* Creating visualizations to communicate findings.
* Validating assumptions before drawing conclusions.
* Interpreting analytical results.
* Using data to support business decisions.

## What I Learned

One of my key learnings was that the quality of an analysis depends heavily on the quality of the data being analyzed.

I learned that data cleaning and preparation are important steps before performing analysis. I also learned how descriptive statistics can help summarize data and how visualizations can make patterns and trends easier to understand.

Another important lesson was that analysis should connect back to a real question or business problem. The goal is not simply to calculate numbers, but to use those numbers to support better decisions.

## Challenges and Learnings

Working with spreadsheets helped me become more comfortable with structured data and analytical thinking.

I also learned to pay attention to errors, inconsistencies, missing information, and assumptions that could affect the results of an analysis.

This stage helped me understand the importance of checking and validating my work before using analytical results to make decisions.

## Highlights

A major highlight of this stage was learning how to move from **raw data to meaningful insights**.

This was an important step in my development because it gave me practical experience with the core workflow of data analysis.

## Next Step

Move into **SQL and Databases** to learn how to work with structured data stored in databases and develop stronger skills for querying and analyzing larger datasets.
