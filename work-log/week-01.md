# Week 01 — Starting My ALX Data Analytics Journey

## Summary

This week marked the beginning of my journey into Data Analytics with ALX.

I started by exploring the ALX learning environment and becoming familiar with the structure of the program. I was introduced to the importance of documenting my learning journey and building practical skills that can eventually be demonstrated to employers.

I then moved into the **Data and AI Literacy Foundation**, where I began developing a basic understanding of data, artificial intelligence, and the relationship between data and AI.

## Achievements

* Started my ALX Data Analytics journey.
* Became familiar with the ALX learning environment and course structure.
* Started the Data and AI Literacy Foundation.
* Learned the basic concepts of data and the importance of data in decision-making.
* Began understanding the role of AI in modern data analysis.
* Started exploring how AI tools can support data analysis and problem-solving.

## Challenges and Learnings

At the beginning of the program, I encountered many new concepts and terms.

I learned that becoming a data analyst is not only about learning technical tools. It also requires understanding how data is collected, interpreted, communicated, and used to solve real-world problems.

I also began to understand that AI can support data analysis, but an analyst still needs to understand the data, validate results, and make responsible decisions.

## Highlights

My biggest highlight this week was taking the first step toward becoming a data analyst.

Starting the Data and AI Literacy Foundation gave me a broader understanding of the field and prepared me to move into more practical data analysis skills.

## Next Step

Continue with the Data and AI Literacy Foundation and build a strong foundation in data and AI before moving into practical data analysis with spreadsheets.
